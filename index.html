How to Use Puxium
  1. 01 Download and open Puxium.
  2. 02 Launch Roblox and start playing Blade Ball.
  3. 03 In Blade Ball, go to Settings → Block and set your Block keys to:

    Keybinds Block Mouse 1 F F ↩
  4. 04 That's it — Puxium should work properly!

Tip: Using two F block binds is recommended to boost spam speed.

Note: Be sure to read the in-app "info" or "about puxium & informations" by typing io or info at Puxium — it contains usefull informations.
